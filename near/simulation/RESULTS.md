# Floor simulation results

Generated by `cargo test --release -p pop-game write_simulation_results -- --ignored` (source: `near/game/src/tests/sim.rs`). Every step runs the real contract code in near-sdk's mocked runtime. After **every** action the harness asserts that vault ÷ supply did not fall, and an independent ledger of NEAR in and out asserts the contract holds exactly what it owes (vault, open runs, prepaid runs and the creator's unpaid escrow). Any failure stops the run, so these numbers only exist because no step failed.

Params: the standard game (0.02 NEAR fee, 70% to the vault, launch price 0.0001 NEAR, target score 40, wallet cap 500, mining closes at 10,000 coins or the run cap).

## Reading the bot farm

Bots at scores 0-3 mine only 25-31% of each run's budget, and the rest of every fee stays in the vault, so their runs raise the floor for everyone. But mining closes after 300 runs, so a farm can use up the mining phase: here it closed on day 3, and the 60 bot wallets mined 222.9 of the 315.6 coins mined in all. The farm still ended 1.4449 NEAR down after redeeming. The per-wallet cap does not stop one person with many wallets; that is a stated limit, and Phase 6 caps free runs per login.
